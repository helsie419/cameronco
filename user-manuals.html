Dashboard Manual

The dashboard is the daily overview for open quote value, workshop load, unpaid balances, pipeline volume, and urgent work.

How to use it

  1. Open Dashboard from the top navigation.
  2. Review the metric cards for open quote value, workshop load, and unpaid balances.
  3. Use the Pipeline rail to see where work is sitting.
  4. Use the Today panel for jobs or quotes needing attention.
Tip: If the dashboard looks empty, check that the database/API is connected and that claims or jobs have been created.

Customers Manual

The Customers screen is where staff find, review, and open customer records. Customers are no longer added from a popup here — Add customer now takes you to the same full-screen page used to start a quote, since in practice a new customer almost always exists because a new quote is starting.

Find a customer

  1. Open Customers.
  2. Search by name, phone, email, or style notes.
  3. Use the Type and Consent filters when narrowing a long list.
  4. Click a customer row to open the customer record. The phone number shown in the table is also a click-to-call link.

Add a customer

  1. Click Add customer — this opens the full-screen quote entry page (see Creating an Order for the paste-and-extract and duplicate-detection steps that happen there).
  2. Search for the customer first — if a close name/address match already exists, a warning offers to link to that existing customer instead of creating a duplicate.
  3. Enter or confirm the customer's details, then continue into the claim.

Bulk upload customers

  1. Click Bulk upload.
  2. Paste CSV rows using the columns shown in the placeholder.
  3. Click Import customers.
Important: Search before adding a customer. One customer can have many claims, quotes, and jobs.

Customer Record Manual

A customer record opens full-screen (not a popup) so the main navigation stays reachable while you're on it — it shows contact details, consent, work orders, and correspondence.

Update a record

  1. Open the customer from the Customers table.
  2. Save customer and Send correspondence sit together at the top of the page.
  3. Full name, customer type, phone, and email sit on one row; address and preferred contact, ring size and partner/occasion, and lead source follow below.
  4. Edit any field, then click Save customer.
What are Ring size / Partner-occasion for? These are general relationship/marketing fields carried over from the original spreadsheets — not tied to any particular claim. They're useful for retail/private customers (e.g. a future engagement ring purchase) but won't apply to most insurance work, which is why they're optional and sit lower on the page.

Review history

  • Work Orders: every claim/quote for this customer — claim number, whether it's insurance work (and which insurer) or private work, its current status, the date, and the quoted total.
  • Correspondence: shows SiteGround email matches and logged CRM notes.
  • Send correspondence: opens Customer Contact with that customer selected.

Customer Contact Manual

Customer Contact is used to send or log messages and keep a timeline of customer communication.

Send or log a message

  1. Open Customer Contact.
  2. Choose Send email or Log phone call / other communication.
  3. Select one customer, or choose a customer list.
  4. Choose the linked work item if relevant.
  5. Select a template, then review the subject and message body.
  6. Tick Create follow-up task if a follow-up should be created.
  7. Click the submit button to save the communication.
Consent: The CRM suppresses some sends when the customer does not have the correct email, SMS, or marketing consent.

Email History Manual

Email history reads recent matching messages from the configured SiteGround mailbox and displays them on the customer record.

Refresh customer email

  1. Open a customer record.
  2. Check that the customer has an email address.
  3. In Correspondence, click Refresh email.
  4. Review inbound and outbound messages from the mailbox.
Setup required: Netlify must have IMAP environment variables configured before this feature can read SiteGround email.

Quotes Pipeline Manual

The Quotes screen lists every claim or quote in the pipeline.

Use the pipeline

  1. Open Quotes (now the second item in the main navigation, right after Dashboard).
  2. Use search to find a claim number, customer, or insurer.
  3. Filter by status or branch if needed.
  4. Click Open to return to a quote.
  5. If a claim is approved and hasn't been invoiced yet, use Send to Xero to create a draft invoice.
  6. Once it's been sent, the button is replaced by the Xero invoice number — click it to open that invoice directly in Xero (opens in a new tab; you'll need to be logged into the right Xero organisation).
Note: Sending is one-way per claim — once an invoice number is showing, there's no button to send again, so it can't accidentally be double-invoiced.
Note: Job creation happens on the Job Board after the quote/claim is approved.

Creating an Order Manual

In this CRM, an order starts as a quote or claim. Once approved, it becomes a workshop job.

Before you start

  • Customer details
  • Insurer and claim number, if insurance work
  • Item description, metal, stones, dimensions, and costing details
  • Policy limits, excess, salvage, and settlement notes

Create the quote/order

Customer and claim are created together on one full-screen page — there's no separate "add customer" step for insurance work.

  1. Open Quotes and click New quote (or Add customer from the Customers screen — both land on the same page).
  2. Paste from insurer portal: if you have the claim details copied from the insurer's portal, paste the text into the box at the top of the Customer panel and click Extract details. It fills in the customer's name, address, phone, email, and — where present — the claim number, insurer, and reference. This is rule-based text matching, not AI, so always check the fields it fills in before continuing.
  3. Otherwise, search for the customer by name, email, or phone, or type the details in manually.
  4. Possible duplicate: if the name and address closely match an existing customer, a warning banner appears with that customer's name and address. Click Use this existing customer to link to them instead of creating a new record, or dismiss it if it's genuinely a different person.
  5. Enter claim details. For insurance work, enter the insurer's claim number. For private/retail work with no insurer selected, the claim number field greys out and fills itself in automatically from the internal reference below — there's nothing to type there.
  6. Internal reference: a Cameron & Co reference number (e.g. CC-2026-0001) is generated automatically for new claims if you leave "Our reference" blank. Its prefix, digit-padding, yearly reset, and starting number are configurable in Settings > Internal Reference Numbers.
  7. Add each item, including specifications, stones, and costing.
  8. Enter settlement details.
  9. Click Save draft if not ready, or Generate quote when ready.

Internal sign-off, then send

A generated quote sits as Pending approval and cannot be emailed until an admin signs off on it — this catches pricing mistakes before the customer ever sees them.

  1. On the Quotes pipeline, pick yourself under Approving as (admin role required — anyone else is rejected).
  2. Use PDF to check how the quote will look, then click Approve.
  3. Once approved, click Email quote to send it to the customer's email on file.

Approval

Before an order can become a workshop job or a Xero invoice, the quote must separately be marked approved — meaning the customer or insurer has accepted it. This is a different step from the internal sign-off above.

Current note: The backend supports this customer-acceptance status, but the visible screens do not yet show a dedicated button for it. Until that's added, it needs to be set via the API (PUT /api/quotes/:id/status).

Job Board Manual

The Job Board is the workshop view. Approved claims become jobs here, then move through production stages.

Create a job

  1. Open Job Board.
  2. Click Create job from an approved claim.
  3. Find the approved claim.
  4. Click Create job.

Update a job

  1. Click a job card.
  2. Set the stage, owner, start date, due date, and items taken.
  3. Review or update component lines.
  4. Add supplier, stock number, invoice number, weight, rate, and actual cost.
  5. Click Save changes.
Stages Awaiting deposit, CAD approval, In production, Quality check, Ready for collection, Completed.
Margin The job card compares quoted nett with actual component costs entered so far.

Stock Manual

The Stock screen is the ledger for loose stones, metal, ring mounts/configurations, and finished pre-made pieces. While quoting, it shows whether a matching item is already on hand or needs ordering.

Add a stock item

  1. Open Stock.
  2. Click Add stock item.
  3. Pick a category — the form only shows the fields relevant to it.
  4. Enter quantity, unit, and a reorder threshold — the item is flagged "Order needed" once quantity drops to or below that threshold.
  5. Click Save.

Bulk upload stock

  1. Click Bulk upload.
  2. Paste CSV rows using the columns shown in the placeholder (category and description are required; the rest depend on category).
  3. Click Import stock.

Checking stock while quoting

On Quote entry, filling in a stone's type/shape/carat/quality, or an item's metal type/colour/category, shows a small badge next to those fields — green if a match is in stock, amber if it needs ordering. This is informational only: it never changes stock quantities or item pricing.

Note: Finished/pre-made pieces aren't auto-matched while quoting — browse them directly on the Stock screen instead.

Rate Cards Manual

Rate Cards control quoting rates for manufacturing, material, setting, labour, chain, watch brands, and related pricing.

Update a manufacturing or material rate

Every field is click-to-edit directly in the table — there's no separate Edit popup.

  1. Open Rate Cards.
  2. Find the relevant rate group.
  3. Click into Category, Label, Metal, Origin, Rate, or Unit and change it. Changing Category moves the rate into that group's table as soon as you save.
  4. The change saves automatically when you click away from the field — no separate save step.
  5. Click history next to a rate to see every past version and when it was current.
  6. Click Retire to stop offering a rate from today — a confirmation dialog checks first. Retiring never changes a quote that already used that rate.

Add a new rate

  1. Click ＋ Add new rate at the bottom of the rates list, or ＋ Add rate on a specific group's heading to start pre-filled to that category.
  2. Fill in the details and click Save new version.

Bulk update rates from a spreadsheet

  1. Click Download CSV to export every current rate.
  2. Edit values in the spreadsheet, keeping the code column unchanged so each row updates the right rate.
  3. Click Upload CSV, choose the file, and review the preview of what will change.
  4. Click Confirm import.

Add or update a watch brand rate

  1. Use Watch brand rates.
  2. Filter by insurer or search by brand if needed.
  3. Click Add watch brand rate or edit an existing row.
  4. Choose discount percentage or POA.
  5. Click Save new version.
Important: Editing a rate creates a new version from today. Past generated quotes keep the rate that was frozen at the time.

Staff & Roles Manual

Staff records drive the assignment and ownership fields used across claims and jobs — who a claim is Assessed by, and who owns a job on the Job Board.

Add or update a staff member

  1. Open Settings, then Staff.
  2. Enter the name, choose a role (Assessor, Workshop, or Admin) and branch (Melbourne or Sydney).
  3. Tick Can approve quotes if this person should be selectable under "Approving as" on the Quotes pipeline — admin role is required there regardless.
  4. Click Add / update staff.
  5. To change an existing staff member's name, role, branch, or approval permission, edit the value directly in their row — it saves as soon as you change it.

Deactivate a staff member

  1. Open Settings > Staff.
  2. Find their row in the table and click the Active badge in the Status column.
  3. The badge switches to Inactive and the row disappears from the list — nothing is deleted.

Reactivate a staff member

  1. Open Settings > Staff.
  2. Tick Show inactive/past staff to bring inactive rows back into view.
  3. Click their Inactive badge to switch it back to Active.
What deactivating does: an inactive staff member drops out of the "Assessed by" and job "Owner" dropdowns elsewhere in the CRM, but stays attached to any claim or job already assigned to them — nothing on past work changes.

Automations Manual

Automations prepare templated customer emails for key job phases, such as quote follow-up, deposit request, CAD approval, ready for collection, and care reminders.

Create an automation

  1. Open Automation.
  2. Enter the template name.
  3. Select the phase that triggers the send.
  4. Select the timing.
  5. Enter the subject and email body.
  6. Click Save automation.

Run automations

  1. Review Scheduled sends.
  2. Click Run due sends.
  3. Review the Run log.

Reports Manual

Reports provide sales pipeline, workshop stage, data quality, and export audit views.

Use reports

  1. Open Reports.
  2. Choose the role view: Owner, Sales, Workshop, or Finance.
  3. Review the relevant report cards.
  4. Click Export report if an export is needed and permitted.

Troubleshooting Manual

I cannot save a quote

  • Check that a customer has been selected or created.
  • Check that the claim number has been entered.
  • Check that address fields are complete if an address was started.
  • Check that the database/API connection is working.

An approved claim does not appear on the Job Board

  • Check that the quote has been approved.
  • Check that the claim status is approved.
  • Check that a job has not already been created for that claim.

Email history does not load

  • Check that the customer has an email address.
  • Check Netlify IMAP environment variables.
  • Redeploy after changing Netlify environment variables.
  • Check that the SiteGround mailbox password is correct.

Pricing looks wrong

  • Check item weight, metal or chain rate, setting quantity, labour hours, and stone cost.
  • Check policy limits, salvage allocation, and current rate card values.
