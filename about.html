About Us

Cameron & Co is a trusted family-owned business with over 25 years of expertise in jewellery and watches.

We pride ourselves in our Quality craftsmanship, our team works with our clients to design items to your requirements in sterling silver, yellow gold, rose gold, white gold, platinum and set with diamonds, precious gems and semi-precious gems. We deal with reputable diamond and precious gem suppliers and trusted certifiers, such as GIA.

Since our inception, we have and continue to work with major insurance companies to support their clients with their jewellery and watch claims. Our showrooms, and commitment to personalised service distinguish us as reliable insurance specialists and craftsmen. Cameron & Co are renowned for our deep industry knowledge, we are trusted partners with major insurance companies, providing current appraisals, replacement pieces, and exceptional service.

At Cameron & Co, our ethos is rooted in a family legacy of craftsmanship, reliability, and genuine care. We pride ourselves on being a beacon of trust and expertise, fostering enduring relationships with clients and insurance partners alike.

We believe in the artistry of jewellery making, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern design to create timeless pieces.

Mission

Cameron & Co expertly guide and supports clients through the intricate journey of jewellery and watch insurance claims, offering unparalleled craftsmanship, personalised service, and a trusted partnership built on over 25 years of family-owned excellence.

Vision

Core Values

Expertise: We leverage over 25 years of experience to provide unmatched knowledge in the appraisal, replacement, and repair of jewellery and watches.

  • Integrity: We operate with transparency, honesty, and respect in every interaction, ensuring the highest ethical standards are upheld.

  • Personalised Service: We offer a personalised, client-focused approach, understanding the emotional significance of each piece and navigating the insurance claims process with care.
  • Industry Leadership: We strive to be at the forefront of the industry, continuously updating our knowledge and collaborating with reputable suppliers and certifiers such as GIA.
