Insurance Specialists

We help you replace your lost, damaged or stolen jewellery

Working closely with Australia's major insurance companies and loss adjusting firms, Cameron & Co will help you with your lost, damaged or stolen jewellery. For over 25 years, Cameron & Co has worked specifically to assist people with their insurance claims, providing current appraisals of items as well as replacement pieces. Cameron & Co has a proven and trusted reputation with your insurance company for industry knowledge, extensive range of product, design/manufacture and service levels.

For your convenience, we open by appointment Monday to Friday with extended hours should that help with your busy schedule.

What We Can Do For You

Appraisals

Depending on information available, your location, schedule and personal preference, the appraisal process is conducted either by phone, at our showroom or at your home or workplace. For your convenience, we open by appointment Monday to Friday with extended hours should that help with your busy schedule.

Jewellery Remodelling

We can remake your old jewellery into new designs. Our jewellers take apart the original piece and reuse the gems and metals. We then craft a custom, updated jewellery item based on your style.

Repairs

People are advised to get their jewellery checked on a regular basis for wear and tear. A few minor repairs will save you from a loss of your precious stones. At Cameron & Co we offer a complimentary ring clean and will also examine your jewellery for wear and tear. We offer an array of repair services for your jewellery items.

Jewellery Replacements

Once your items have been assessed, a replacement value will be established by your insurance company. You may choose to replace with similar or alternative items and are able to replace with items from stock, special order or individually designed and handcrafted in our workshop.
